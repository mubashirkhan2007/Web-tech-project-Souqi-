01

Engineering

Front-End Web Developer

Build and improve the pages our customers use every day, from product listings to checkout, with a focus on speed, accessibility and clean code.

Reference number
SQF01
Salary
QAR 15,000 to QAR 22,000 per month (tax-free) plus allowances
Reports to
Head of Digital Engineering
Location and type
Doha, Qatar, full-time, hybrid
Responsibilities and requirements

Key responsibilities

  • Build and maintain product listing, product detail and checkout pages using semantic HTML5 and CSS3
  • Turn designers' wireframes into responsive pages for mobile, tablet and desktop
  • Make sure every page meets web accessibility standards (WCAG)
  • Improve page load speed for product listings and checkout
  • Manage code changes with Git and GitHub and review teammates' code
  • Work with the security team to protect customer details entered in forms

Requirements

Essential

  • Degree in IT or computer science, or equivalent work experience
  • At least 2 years of front-end development experience
  • Strong skills in HTML5, CSS3 and responsive design
  • Experience using Git for version control in a team

Preferable

  • Experience working on an e-commerce or online retail website
  • Experience using Jira in an agile team
  • An online portfolio or GitHub profile showing your work

02

Design & UX

UX/UI Designer

Shape how customers browse, search and buy on Souqi by researching their needs and designing clear, inclusive shopping journeys.

Reference number
SQU02
Salary
QAR 14,000 to QAR 20,000 per month (tax-free) plus allowances
Reports to
Product Design Lead
Location and type
Doha, Qatar, full-time, hybrid
Responsibilities and requirements

Key responsibilities

  • Research customer needs through interviews, surveys and usability testing
  • Design wireframes and prototypes for browsing, search, cart and checkout
  • Keep the colours, fonts and components of Souqi's design system consistent
  • Check that designs meet accessibility standards, including colour contrast
  • Work with developers so designs are built accurately

Requirements

Essential

  • Degree in design or a related field, or equivalent experience
  • At least 2 years designing websites or apps
  • Strong skills in Figma or a similar design tool
  • A portfolio showing your design process, not only finished screens

Preferable

  • Experience designing for e-commerce or online retail
  • Basic knowledge of HTML and CSS
  • Experience designing for culturally and linguistically diverse customers
