About Souqi

Good things start with good people.

We’re building a more thoughtful way to shop online, bringing people and good finds a little closer together.

In a traditional souq, the seller knows the goods and the shopper knows what they’re hoping to find. The best part is the conversation in between. Souqi is our attempt to keep that conversation going on a screen.

That starts with the team behind it. We like curious minds, honest questions and people who care about getting the small details right.
